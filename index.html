Humanico Voice

state-of-the-art-for-1980 speech synthesis, running entirely in your browser
AN ORIGINAL IMPLEMENTATION of Dennis H. Klatt's 1980 cascade/parallel formant synthesizer (JASA 67(3)), written from the published paper. No samples, no neural nets, no code copied from any existing synth. 10 kHz, the way it sounded when the future was new.

Inspired by klattsch (tgies.github.io/klattsch) and its not-yet-released plugin. This is our own engine - and our own plugin is coming.
Bots draw perfect lines. People wobble beautifully. HUMANICO
